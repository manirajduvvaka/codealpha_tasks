# Phishing Awareness Knowledge Check

### 1. An unexpected message says your account will be closed in 10 minutes. What should you do?
**Answer:** Open the official site/app directly and verify the alert. Urgency is a common social-engineering pressure.

### 2. What should you inspect in a sender address?
**Answer:** Check the complete address and the genuine registrable domain; words such as “secure” or “login” in a domain do not make it trustworthy.

### 3. Does HTTPS prove that a website is legitimate?
**Answer:** No. HTTPS protects the connection; it does not prove the site itself is trustworthy.

### 4. What is smishing?
**Answer:** Phishing delivered through SMS or another messaging channel.

### 5. What should you do with a suspicious attachment?
**Answer:** Do not open it. Report it through the approved organizational process and follow security-team guidance.
