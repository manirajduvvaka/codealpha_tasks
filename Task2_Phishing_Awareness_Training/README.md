## Key message
**Pause → Inspect → Verify → Report**

Never enter credentials, OTPs, card details or recovery codes into a page reached from an unexpected message. Verify sensitive requests through a trusted channel.

Examples are fictional/defensive and do not include working credential-harvesting pages or malicious links.
